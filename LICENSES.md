- OpenAL Soft, Oboe, libdwarf, Ogg/Vorbis, libpng, IJG JPEG and the Android C++ runtime: retain their own LGPL, Apache, BSD and other component-specific notices as applicable.
- NZ:P game assets: retain the upstream attribution file and component terms. The asset collection is generally CC BY-SA 4.0, with credited exceptions including CC BY 3.0, CC0 and CC BY-SA 3.0.

Complete applicable license texts, original attribution, exact source revisions and modification notices must accompany publication of the corresponding material. The release review must verify the inventory before any APK is distributed.
